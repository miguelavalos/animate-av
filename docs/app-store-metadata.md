# Animate AV App Store Metadata

This document is intentionally public-safe.

Working App Store metadata, ASO drafts, screenshot captions, subscription copy,
pricing language, and launch positioning are private product/release material
and must be maintained in the private AVALSYS suite.

## Public Rule

Do not add final or draft App Store Connect values here, including:

- subtitles, keywords, descriptions, promotional text, or screenshot captions;
- product pricing, credit-pack language, Pro benefits, or subscription terms;
- launch regions, review strategy, promo codes, or campaign copy;
- claims that have not been verified against the submitted build and legal copy.

The public repo may include only frontend-safe setup and contribution docs.
