# Animate AV App Privacy Inventory

This document is intentionally public-safe.

The working App Privacy inventory is private release/legal material. It must be
kept in the private AVALSYS suite because it can include backend storage
behavior, provider processing, retention operations, diagnostics, purchase
reconciliation, account deletion consequences, and App Store Connect answers.

## Public Rule

Do not add internal privacy analysis, provider/model terms, retention runbooks,
admin behavior, purchase ledgers, App Store Connect answers, or legal drafts to
this public repository.

Public-facing privacy commitments should appear only in the published Privacy
Policy and in shipped app copy after legal/privacy review.
